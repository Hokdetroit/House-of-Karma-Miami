Open positions

Healthcare branding & websites

Build trust before the first appointment. Healthcare brand strategy, identity, patient-facing websites, conversion-focused landing pages, content production, reputation management, and referral campaigns work together as one acquisition system.

EMR, dashboards & telehealth

We architect custom EMR systems, EHR integrations, secure patient dashboards, intake flows, appointment experiences, and referral portals around your clinical workflow. Integration scope, access controls, data migration, testing, and vendor permissions are defined before development.

HIPAA & policy readiness

Connect discovery, consent, scheduling, secure intake, virtual visits, and follow-up in a coordinated telehealth experience. Clinical delivery and patient data require approved vendors, appropriate BAAs, role-based access, and a practice-specific compliance review.

Paid ads & healthcare SEO

Healthcare Google Ads, paid social, local SEO, technical SEO, service-page content, and conversion optimization connect qualified demand to your practice. We evaluate policy eligibility, consent, and measurement boundaries before launching campaigns; PHI must not enter advertising platforms.

CRM, automation & support

Studio Escrow powers the CRM and automation layer: secure intake, patient communications, scheduling, referral tracking, audit logs, and role-based access. HIPAA-compliant operation depends on the contracted Medical Suite, signed BAAs, appropriate configuration, and your organization’s policies and training.
