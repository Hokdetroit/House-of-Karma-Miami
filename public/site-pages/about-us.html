Mohamad founded House of Karma Miami to bring thoughtful business strategy, authentic relationships, creative execution, and scalable systems together. His approach starts with understanding people—not choosing a marketing platform.

He studied Business & Marketing at the University of Michigan and brings more than a decade of finance and sales experience, including serving as Director of Banking at Rocket Mortgage, where he coached and developed sales professionals.

As founder of Karma by Design, he also explores art, storytelling, composition, and brand identity. At House of Karma Miami, those business and creative disciplines inform the strategy behind each major engagement.
